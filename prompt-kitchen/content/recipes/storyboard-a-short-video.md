Most short videos don't fail at filming; they fail at the blank page before it. This recipe turns "I should make a video about X" into a numbered shot list you can film on a phone — or paste, shot by shot, into a video generator.

## The Recipe

```
Turn this idea into a storyboard for a [30/60]-second vertical video: [IDEA].
Audience: [WHO IT'S FOR]. Platform: [Shorts / Reels / TikTok / an AI video tool].

Structure:
- Hook (first 2 seconds): the shot or line that stops the scroll. No intro, no logo, no "hey guys".
- 3–5 beats that build to one payoff.
- Ending: [loop back to the hook / one clear call to action].

For each shot give me:
- Shot number and duration in seconds
- What's on screen — specific enough to film, or to paste into a video generator as the shot's prompt
- The exact words: spoken line, or on-screen text if nobody talks
- Sound: music cue, sound effect, or silence

Rules: total runtime must fit the target. If any shot can't be done by one person with a phone, flag it and give a simpler alternative.
```

## Example — A 30-second "one tip" video

Idea: *the two-minute trick that rescues over-salted soup.* The storyboard comes back as: Shot 1 (0–2s) — close-up of a grimacing taste-test, on-screen text "Too salty? Don't add water." Shot 2 (2–9s) — drop in a raw potato wedge, spoken line "A potato pulls salt as it simmers." Shot 3 (9–22s) — simmer, stir, second taste, text overlay "12 minutes". Shot 4 (22–30s) — the nod, the bowl lifted to camera, line "Fixed. Follow for the next rescue." Four shots, one phone, no excuses left.

## Tips for better results

- Write the hook last. Once the beats exist, the strongest opening line is usually hiding in beat three — move it to second zero.
- One idea per video. If the storyboard needs a second payoff, that's your next video, not this one's second half.
- For AI video tools, each "what's on screen" line *is* the generation prompt — ask for them in the generator's language (subject, action, camera move, light) and generate shot by shot, not all at once.
- Read the spoken lines aloud and time yourself. Scripts always run longer spoken than they look written; the storyboard's seconds are a promise your mouth has to keep.

## 🔁 Leftover Remixes

**🌶️ Spicy:** *"Give me three different hooks for this same storyboard — the safe one, the bold one, and the weird one — and rank them for my audience."*

**🧊 Mild:** Beats only: *"Break [IDEA] into 4 beats for a 30-second video. No timings, no script — just what happens in each beat."*

**💰 Budget:** *"Here's a blog post I already wrote: [PASTE]. Storyboard it — pull the hook and beats from the post, don't invent new material."*
