Alterac Mountains census (pack 0.34.0)

Contested zone: the census keys on Alliance accepts. Horde content is counted and set aside for the Horde block's own census.

  • Accept-zone key used: 1416 (Alterac Mountains; Alterac Valley is the separate battleground map 1459). First pull keyed 1426 by mistake (Dun Morogh's key) and was discarded; the 1416 pull below is the real census.
  • Total candidates: 164 zone-field records. A starter sweep (dual-spawn 1416 NPCs/objects with zone fields elsewhere) found 2 touches, both Thousand Needles quests starting from Bath'rah (79361, 79364) — they stay with their home zone under the accept rule.
  • Band derivation (quest level draws the low line, minLevel the top): IN-level quests run 33–40 (510/511 at 34, 603 at 37, 96800 at 38, 97130/1712/1713/1792/79365 at 40); a clean break sits below 30 (next-lowest Alliance level is 27) and the finales run 41/45/46 with minLevels 34/37/39. Band: 30–40. Above-band OUT: m44+ (96609, 80411, 80526, 8234, 96603, 97173, 96772, 96797) plus the level-60 donation chain and the unresolvable 96602 (see BORDERLINE). Below-band OUT: quest levels 13/24/25/27 (94947, 6681, 96779, 79362, 79363, 96137, 94912).
  • IN: 20 (20 accepts, 19 turn-ins — 96783 is an accept-only record with no ender, contracted with an honest note per the Ironforge precedent). OUT: 144 = 67 Horde + 47 Alterac Valley battleground + 4 repeatable + 2 seasonal + 1 broken/unplaceable + 8 above-band + 7 below-band + 4 donation chain + 1 pre-chain-above-band + 3 accept-outside (Silverpine).
  • 512/514 debt cleared: 510 (Foreboding Plans, object-start, ends Maleb) feeds Hillsbrad 512, and 511 (Encrypted Letter, object-start, ends Dibbs) feeds Hillsbrad 514 → 525 (Ironforge, contracted) → 537 (Hillsbrad, contracted). Both Hillsbrad chains now read end to end.

The zone field (1416) loses to the operational accept test per the Redridge dual-spawn precedent; objectives in-zone with accept elsewhere are OUT by the stated rule.
