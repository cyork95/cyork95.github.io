Those quests belong to the future Undercity city census, NOT this contract.

Method: every quest with an accept/start touching 1420 - start NPC spawning on 1420, start object spawning on 1420, or start item whose drop source spawns on 1420 - was enumerated (175 candidates), then filtered by the pack rules. Item-starts are enumerated explicitly below (the Mulgore lesson); no absence is asserted without a start-item scan.

Band 1-13: lowest contracted minLevel 1 to highest contracted quest level 13 (99156 Rear Guard Patrol, min6 - finale rule, same shape as Durotar/Mulgore). The minLevel cluster is dense 1-10 (96 candidates), then 12 (the 5-quest Divinity paladin line), then a gap: min15+ quests are chains whose working levels belong to the 10-20 bracket and are OUT by the minLevel line.
