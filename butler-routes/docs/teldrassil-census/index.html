Borderline

(a) 934 Crown of the Earth vs 7383 Crown of the Earth - RECOMMEND: 7383 IN, 934 OUT

Same-name twins, mutually exclusive (each lists the other in excl), identical chain slot (both pre 933, both next 935, which takes either). The data breaks the tie by fill location: 934 fills the Vial of Blessed Water at the Temple of the Moon Fountain (city, 1457), 7383 fills the Amethyst Phial at the Oracle Glade Moonwell (in-zone, 38.4,34.0). The in-zone variant fits the Teldrassil route; the city variant belongs with the deferred Darnassus content. Contract 7383.

(b) Priest variants: Laurna (Dolanaar) vs Alathea (city) - DECIDED: Laurna IN, Alathea OUT

Two same-name pairs are split by giver: Stars of Elune 5629 (Laurna) vs 5627 (Alathea, city), Desperate Prayer 5636 (Laurna, Dwarf) vs 5640 (Alathea, Dwarf, city). Mutual excl confirms the variants. The in-zone starter is chain-consistent (matches the 94824/94817 Dun Morogh precedent and the brief’s city rule); Alathea’s versions ride with Darnassus. Human 94775 and Gnome 94823 also start with Laurna - all four Priest lines route through the Dolanaar hub.

(c) Warrior intro 1684 vs city chain 1683/1686/1692/1693 - DECIDED: 1684 IN, rest OUT

1684 starts with Amara/Kyra in Dolanaar and ends at Elanaria (Darnassus, allowed); 1683 starts with Elanaria in the city and the chain continues there (1686, 1692, 1693 all city accepts). 1684 excludes 1683, confirming the variant split. The Teldrassil route takes the in-zone intro as a completable Dolanaar-to-city handoff; the city chain is deferred Darnassus content.

(d) Hunter’s Path 6072 vs 6071 - DECIDED: 6072 IN, 6071 OUT

Same-name twins, mutually exclusive; 6072 starts with Ayanna Everstride in Shadowglen (in-zone), 6071 with Jocaste in the city. The 6073/6721/6722 variants accept in Stormwind/Ironforge/Dun Morogh (other zones’ versions, not candidates). Shadowglen wins by the city rule; check Elwynn’s routing for the cross-zone precedent in the guide phase.
