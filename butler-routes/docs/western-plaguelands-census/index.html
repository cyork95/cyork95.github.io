BORDERLINE

  1. Below-band letters + min-1 keeper. The six Front letters (min 45) sit below the contracted cluster low of 50; 9247 (min 1) is the writ special class. All seven are item-started world content with in-zone turn-ins at the Keeper of the Rolls, unrepeatable, unseasonal, and unbroken. The band rule only excludes minLevel-above-band; nothing in the pack rules excludes below-band item-starts, and the completionist principle (every quest in its zone) favors contracting real obtainable quests over a tidy band floor. IN, band stays 50-58, argued here instead of silently.
  2. Cap-content exclusion. The 98 min-60 quests are real quests, but they are the level-60 endgame turn-in systems (tier sets, attunements, Icebane, Kel'Thuzad, priest line), a full tier above the zone's 50-58 leveling band. The band rule excludes minLevel-above-band; contracting them would make the band 50-60 and misrepresent the zone. OUT, argued here instead of silently.
  3. Broken Argent chain (97986/98109/97987). The head's pre (8152) does not exist in the data, the middle has null minLevel and a spawnless accept NPC, so no player can ever start the chain - unlike missing-objective cases (bare complete + note), a missing pre-chain cannot be routed around. All three OUT; 97987 is not salvageable via soft-pre because its pre can never be onquest.
  4. Spawnless-but-contracted objectives (5212, 5213, 5214, 5529, 5262, 5263, 5206, 5513, 5517, 9141, 84318, 6026/8947/5247 mats). The quest records are complete and their accepts/turn-ins are placed; only the objective items/mobs lack spawns. Per the Jammal'an precedent these ride as bare completes/collects with honest notes, NOT as exclusions - the alternative (dropping 15 quests over data-build spawn gaps) would gut the zone. A first audit pass mis-sliced two NPC IDs (string-offset bug) and briefly made 11064/12247 look like wrong-map mobs; both were re-queried raw and confirmed clean (Darrowshire Spirit and Scourge Structure spawn on 1423 as named). 11122 Restless Soul is genuinely spawnless everywhere in the data, so 5282 rides bare with its note.
  5. No mutual-exclusivity decisions were needed: every excl twin of a contracted quest (5401/5405, 5504/5507, 5521/5524, 9121/9122/9123) accepts outside the zone or above the band. No same-name twin IDs both accept in-zone. No dungeon-accept quests beyond the Strat/Scholo group, both handled at data-placed entrances/markers.
  6. Strat-mirror honesty: the four "Stratholme" objects are named for the instance but carry no quest linkage; the guide uses them as the in-zone face of the Strat run and says exactly that in the step notes, rather than inventing an entrance or leaving the three Strat quests bare.
