  • Retherokk, Witherheart and Zul'arek also drop 9259 Troll Tribal Necklace, the objective item of core-contracted 2880/2881 (Troll Necklace Bounty) - the steps carry a note pointing at the core guide and never accept. Mith'rethis drops it too; his step carries the same note.
  • Razortalon, Jalinde, Mith'rethis and Zul'arek drop 8704 OOX-09/HL Distress Beacon, the starter of core-contracted 485 (Find OOX-09/HL!) - noted, never accepted in the companion.
  • Retherokk, Witherheart, Zul'arek and Mith'rethis drop 9294 Recipe: Wildvine Potion - the recipes companion owns that hunt; cross-noted.
  • Jalinde Summerdrake is faction=A (Alliance-aligned). She is shipped like any other rare: the data flags her rare with loot, and the guide takes no position on whether to attack beyond stating the alignment.
  • Mith'rethis (52) is 1 level above the band top: shipped with an honest come-back-later note (Westfall/Hillsbrad precedent). Nothing is far-above-band, so nothing is excluded on level.
  • No rare here is a giver, ender or objective of any core-contracted quest, so the Dun Morogh exclusion does not fire on any of the 10.
  • TAME: no tame lines ship. The data has no tameability flag. Old Cliff Jumper (wolf: Red Wolf Meat / Tender Wolf Meat) and Ironback (turtle: Turtle Meat) have beast-drop evidence, but the core guide never sends the player at wolves or turtles (core kills are sludges, Witherbark trolls, Soothsayers, rocs, bears, apes, Skylord) - no corroboration. Razortalon has bird-drop evidence (Giant Egg, feathers) and the core has an optional roc kill (2659 Razorbeak Skylord), but the family link is unverifiable from the data. All three logged in docs/companion-unverified.md.
  • Respawn timers are unconfirmed in the data - none stated.
