Thunder Bluff city census (pack 0.98.0 round)

Every quest in the local data (questie-forever, --product wowf + QuestieDB) whose accept/start is in Thunder Bluff (city key 1456, Thunder Bluff areaId 1638): 155 candidates, enumerated by 1456-spawning NPCs (185 NPCs spawn on the city map; script mg-tbcensus.py, raw list C:/tmp/mg-tbcands.txt). No level band (city census): span is quest level 3-60 (min-levels 3-60). Zero item-starts: no candidate has an item start, and no pure-item-start quest anywhere in the data maps to 1456.

Disposition: 78 IN, 77 OUT. The contract (docs/quest-contracts.json, slug thunder-bluff-city, order 50, span 3-60) holds exactly the 78. Accept-only: exactly one IN quest has no turn-in anywhere in the data (92343, documented below); every other IN quest has an NPC or object turn-in.

Method

Accept/start decides: an NPC start with a 1456 spawn is a Thunder Bluff quest even where the giver dual-spawns on the Mulgore map (1412+1456 = city NPC per the city rule). A quest that merely turns in here but accepts elsewhere is not a Thunder Bluff quest (noted, not contracted). Shared multi-city accepts go the pack's usual way: Stormwind/Ironforge/Darnassus/ Orgrimmar each contract their own accepts of the same quests (8151, 8250, 8417, 7492, 1718, 1947/1953 in several cities), so Thunder Bluff contracts its own accepts here. Same-name same-chain twins both route where both accept here (1195/1196 Sacred Flame, 76156/76160/76240 Stalk). The cloth donation chain contracts first-time donations only (7820/7821/7822/7823); Additional Runecloth (7825) is the repeatable tail and stays out, mirroring all prior city censuses.

Reconciliation with the Mulgore 150

The Mulgore census set aside 150 quests to this city under the city rule. 150 of the 155 candidates carry 1412 in their maps, matching that pool; the 5 that do not are 76156/76160/76240 (the Stalk With The Earthmother chain, 1456-only), 97485 (Traditions of the Bluff, 1456-only), and 97022 (dual 1456+1458, the Undercity paladin chain's Thunder Bluff leg). Of the 150: 73 are IN here, 77 are OUT with reasons below. The Mulgore census's full ID list does not survive in the repo, so the reconciliation is by named class, and every named Mulgore-census class checks out:

  • Warsong/Arathi/Alterac battleground monthlies and turn-ins (the 8367-8443 and 98887-98891 blocks): present in the 155, OUT here as repeatables (38 records).
  • Winter Veil / Hallow's End / Lunar Festival / Midsummer / Brewfest / Children's Week seasonals, the 8842/8843 vendor quests, the Darkmoon 7948/... rows: present, OUT as seasonal/event (31 records).
  • The Scepter chain (7484-7488, gated behind the player-unfriendly AQ line): correctly absent from the 155 (Mulgore ruled it OUT itself).
  • Durotar-owned legs (1505 Veteran Uzzek, 1883 Speak with Un'thuwa - accept on Durotar NPCs): present, OUT here (Durotar contracts them; Mulgore listed them as Durotar-owned with the same evidence).
  • Broken/incomplete (60860 no accept anywhere; 97378 next-link with no quest record): present, OUT.
  • Same-name priest variant pairs (5642/5680 Shadowguard, 5644/5646 Dark Sacrifice, 5656/5655 Hex of Weakness, 5663/5661 Touch of Weakness): the Thunder Bluff halves present, OUT here - content homed in Orgrimmar (5680/5646) and Mulgore (5655/5661) respectively; see BORDERLINE.

Two Alliance-only records (8993, 9365) are listed as set-asides inside their seasonal rows.
