Horde city recipes inventory (pack 0.100.0)

Vendor-sold recipes, patterns, plans and schematics whose SOURCE is physically inside the three Horde capitals, verified against the local Forever game data (questie-forever item and NPC vendor tables). This is the contract the three Horde city recipes companions were built against. No city vendor sells a recipe with a limited-stock flag anywhere in the data (item and NPC records carry no stock-class field at all), so no step is marked limited. Seasonals (Winter Veil via the three Copperpinch vendors) ship with an event note. Quest-reward recipes are unverifiable (quest records carry no reward data) — see docs/companion-unverified.md; nothing shippable was found there.

Profession calls follow the vendor's supply specialty (subName), with item-name evidence deciding the mixed-supply vendors (the three Supply Officers, Darah the Recipe Vendor, Hagrus the Reagents Vendor, Daniel Bartlett the General Trade Supplier) and the genuinely mixed Mahu (Leatherworking & Tailoring Supplies). Two standing exceptions, both held over from the Alliance city round: Pattern: Enchanted Mageweave Pouch (22307) is a Tailoring pattern no matter which enchanting vendor stocks it, and Pattern: Phoenix Bindings (211247) names no profession anywhere in the data and ships ungated with an honest note. Fish recipes are Cooking no matter whether the vendor's sign says Cooking or Fishing (Alliance Catherine Leland precedent). Pawani's leather AND mail patterns all ship as Leatherworking: her sign says Leatherworker and every item is a Pattern, the two signals agreeing (mail armor takes the vendor's trade here, not the Blacksmithing Plans prefix it does not carry).

Excluded

  • Zero Alliance-only vendors in scope: every in-city recipe vendor reads faction H (Horde) or AH (neutral, all three Copperpinches) in the data. The faction rule fires on nothing — logged as checked, not assumed.
  • Trainer-taught staples: out everywhere (Polestar's own profession guides cover progression).
  • Quest-reward recipes: unverifiable class — quest records carry no reward data, so no candidate can be confirmed or denied; nothing ships (see docs/companion-unverified.md).
  • Dropped-only recipes: none in scope — every recipe item with a city vendor link ships via that vendor regardless of other drop sources (the Alliance round's city rule: vendor sale decides).
  • Everything else a city vendor sells (armor, weapons, reagents, trade goods): not recipes, out of scope.
  • Multi-point vendors (Jornah 4, Dokimi 4, Gishah 6, Angela Ward 7 spawn points on their city keys): one vendor step each, pinned to the first-listed data point, which matches the census-reported points where the census gave them.
