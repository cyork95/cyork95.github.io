Method (settled): a quest belongs to the zone if its accept/start is on 1428. Chains ending outside are IN; objectives in-zone with accept elsewhere are OUT. The giver-spawn test was run on every giver; dual-spawn givers follow the Tirion precedent (EPL census): a giver spawning on the key keeps the quest here (Corporal Keeshan 349 dual-spawns 1428+1433, so Missing in Action stays BS by the accept-zone rule; Yuka Screwspigot, Kibler, Lothos Riftwaker and Bodley dual/triple-spawn onto 1428, so their quests are candidates here).
