City recipes inventory (pack 0.18.0)

Vendor-sold recipes, patterns, plans and schematics whose SOURCE is physically inside the three Alliance capitals, verified against the local Forever game data (questie-forever item and vendor tables). This is the contract the three city recipes companions were built against. No city vendor sells a recipe with a limited-stock flag anywhere in the data, so no step is marked limited. Seasonals ship with an event note. Quest-reward recipes are unverifiable (quest records carry no reward data) — see docs/companion-unverified.md; nothing shippable was found there.

Profession calls follow the census breakdowns (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus census sections): vendor supply specialty decides, with the two standing exceptions — Pattern: Enchanted Mageweave Pouch (22307) and Pattern: Light Leather Reagent Bag (277716) are Tailoring patterns no matter which supply vendor stocks them. Pattern: Phoenix Bindings (211247) and the Argent Training Manual (233197) name no profession anywhere in the data and ship ungated with an honest note.

Excluded

  • Trainer-taught staples: out everywhere (Polestar's own profession guides cover progression).
  • Quest-reward recipes: unverifiable class — quest records carry no reward data, so no candidate can be confirmed or denied; nothing ships (see docs/companion-unverified.md).
  • Dropped-only recipes: none found in scope — every recipe item with a city vendor link ships via that vendor regardless of other drop sources.
  • Everything else a city vendor sells (armor, weapons, reagents, bandages from non-city vendors, holiday fireworks without recipe stock): not recipes, out of scope.
