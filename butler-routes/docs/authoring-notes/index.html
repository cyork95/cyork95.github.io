The taming chain — a correction to the brief

The brief expected the hunter taming chain to sit outside the 142. It doesn't: all five quests are in the tracker list, and ForeverDB's quest pages verify the full chain, in order:

94007 Taming the Beast (accept Elayaa Easewind, Shen'dar hunter trainer 45.2, 44.3 → hand in to Quel'ana Quickgale, Valanaar 59.6, 72.6) → 94978 part 1 of 3 (tame a Windsong Crawler; objective area 51.7, 72.5) → 94979 part 2 of 3 (tame an Ornery Galestrider, Gustberry Lowlands 57.0, 72.1) → 94013 part 3 of 3 (tame a Vuldren Alpha, Gustberry Lowlands 54.1, 75.1) → 94050 Training the Beast (Quel'ana Quickgale). The guide uses complete <id> @x,y live for the three tames — the only complete lines in the guide, because these are the only objectives with player-recorded coordinates I could verify. The level-2 hunter quest 92482 The Way of the Hunter (from Rorian the Dayseeker, like every class's first lesson) is in the class-introduction step.

Ordering basis

  • Hub flow and giver order: ForeverDB's Zephras Isle guide (Thendal's 12-step giver sequence, Shen'dar, Falaath, Valanaar to level 10; NPC coordinates throughout are ForeverDB's "seen in Forever" positions).
  • Levels, faction tags, chain lists: HardcoreHelper's Zephras Isle guide (110 unique quests by hub). Its three named chains are followed exactly: Elemental Unrest → Agitators → Return to Rorian / Aetheen of the Gales → Foul Matriarch → The Next Step; The Missing Scholar ×3 → Fillion's Mission → Catching Wind → Avenged Tenfold → In Service of Zephras; The High Elder's Request → The Turncoat + Al'Aketh Assassins → Unfortunate News → The Cult's True Plans → Desperate Times → Prepare for Battle → Making Our Move.
  • Real completion order: Skyrinis's Dossier biography (78 quest turn-ins, 2026-11-05 – 11-08). It fixed several placements: Camping 101 turn-ins spread across the Shen'dar stay (Cooking at arrival; First Aid / Fishing / Herbalism after Falaath), The Adventurer and The Great Outdoors at Shen'dar arrival, Al'Aketh Assassins completed in the Falaath phase, Unfortunate News turned in immediately after The Turncoat.
  • Quest-page verification (ForeverDB): the five taming quests and the three Missing Scholar quests (92727 → 92849 → 92850, givers and objective areas), which also fixes Fillion's Mission (99260) after part 3.

Coverage reconciliation: the tracker list (142) = HardcoreHelper's 110 − 5 + 37. The 37 are: 23 class quests, the 14 Camping 101 profession quests, The Adventurer, The Great Outdoors, and More Al'Aketh Ears. The 5 HardcoreHelper quests not in the tracker list — Coming of Age, Harmony in Balance, Infestation Investigation, Hopping Helpers, Valanaar Vintages — are therefore not in this guide (the first three are flagged in a step-1 note so a fresh character still does them). If the tracker list is ever extended, extend the guide to match.

Design choices

  • ID-only accept/turnin lines everywhere else: the compiler fills titles, givers and coordinates from its reference data, per the format docs. Player-facing -- notes carry the giver names and coordinates I verified.
  • coords live header: every typed coordinate came from ForeverDB's in-game recordings (the client's own map frame); Zephras Isle is a new zone with no Era frame to fall back on.
  • Other classes'/orders' quests are included, gated (e.g. accept 92466 inside an only Shaman,Horde step; per-line only Mage etc.). This satisfies the 142-quest contract inside an Alliance-headed guide while staying invisible to characters they aren't for.
  • Camping 101 quests are optional (except Herbalism 97968 and Mining 97970 — this route's own professions): Skyrinis's biography shows turn-ins for exactly the five professions he had trained (Cooking, First Aid, Fishing, Herbalism, Mining), which strongly suggests each is offered only once that profession is learned. Tailoring and Enchanting each have two IDs — treated as base + specialisation versions.
  • Mining/Herbalism use train goals, not task: Shen'dar Village is the first hub with profession trainers (ForeverDB's Thendal tables list none), so the training step sits at Shen'dar arrival, marked optional so non-gatherers can skip it. Trainers named in the tip: Messana Crestwind (Mining, 44.8, 44.5), Halassa Fernbreeze (Herbalism, 43.0, 43.5).
  • Fernfeather is an optional task in the Windfield Orchard step (spawns 52.6, 81.2 and 48.0, 85.4, per the brief); Slydris is a note in step 1 (no recorded spawn points).
