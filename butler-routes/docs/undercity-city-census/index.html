Undercity city census

City key 1458 verified by counting: 124 quest records carry zone=1458, and 235 start-NPC refs land on 1458 (reproduces the Tirisfal census counts; the Tirisfal census keyed the open zone on 1420 with 159 zone records and 171 start refs). The city rule applies as in Orgrimmar (1454) and Thunder Bluff (1456): a giver spawning on BOTH the open-zone key (1420) and the city key (1458) is a city NPC, even where the 1420 point sits on the open map.

Method: every quest with an accept/start touching 1458 - start NPC spawning on 1458, start object spawning on 1458, or start item whose source spawns on 1458 - was enumerated (240 UC-linked records), then filtered by the pack rules. Item-starts are enumerated explicitly below (the Mulgore lesson); no absence is asserted without a start-item scan. Zone field alone never decides: 84395-84402 key zone 1453 but start from Oran Snakewrithe (1458) and are contracted here; 357/366/446 key zone 1420 but start from dual-spawn Bethor Iceshard and are contracted here. Conversely 9265/9154 key zone 1458 but are event-gated and OUT.

No level band (city rule): the contract is every quest accepting on 1458, span 1-60 (lowest minLevel 1, the library books; highest level 60, the donation/event-era records).
