They are alternative versions of one quest: 94824 (Maxan Anvol to High Priestess Mims) continues the Kharanos priest chain 5626-5625, while 94817 (Mims to Mims) is the standalone variant. Only one can ever complete, so only one can sit in the contract. Recommendation: contract 94824 (chain-consistent), keep 94817 OUT as the unpicked variant. If the pilot shows Gnome Priests are actually offered 94817 first, swap them.
