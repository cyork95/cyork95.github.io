Winterspring census (pack butler-routes-completionist)

Census from local Forever game data (questie-forever quests/npcs/objects/items.json), built with --product wowf + QuestieDB. Accept-zone key 1452 (verified: the map key carrying Everlook, Donova Snowden, Jaron Stoneshaper, Wynd Nightchaser, Ranshalla, Haleh, Lorax, the Donova/Winterfall mob sets and the Kel'Theril ruin objects; 78 giver/object candidates accept here, plus 4 item-start discoveries).

Method (settled): a quest belongs to the zone if its accept/start is on 1452 — giver spawn, start object, or an item whose drop source spawns in-zone. Chains ending outside are IN; objectives in-zone with accept elsewhere are OUT. Item-started quests (including rare-drop starts) are IN. Race masks: null and Alliance neutral IN; Horde-only OUT (set aside below). No class, skill, event, pre, excl or next field anywhere bars an IN quest from gating — gates are per-line in the guide.

Band: 47-60

Lowest contracted minLevel 47 (Donova's opener pair 3909/3912, genuine early-zone content leading into the 52 cluster) to highest contracted quest level 60. Bulk floor is effectively 50 (ten quests); nothing sits above the band. The six custom quests with null minLevel (levels 56-60) are treated by quest level and sit inside the band — argued in BORDERLINE.

BORDERLINE

  1. Band 47-60. Literal lowest contracted minLevel (47) to highest contracted quest level (60). Bulk floor is 50; the Donova opener pair at 47 is genuine early-zone content, not an outlier, so no outlier machinery. Null-minLevel customs (levels 56-60) treated by quest level, all inside. Nothing above-band.
  2. Repeatables are OUT even where they sting. The E'ko turn-ins (4801-4807), the Rivern chain, the Argent catalogue and the Timbermaw tails are all flagged repeatable in the data — the rule fires regardless of chain charm. The guide notes the E'ko turn-ins at Mau'ari and the Horde-variant 6029 at Gregor so players know what was left out and why.
  3. 6028/6030 vs 6029. Same giver, three faction ends: 6028 ends Alliance, 6029 Horde-locked (races flag), 6030 neutral. Contract the two an Alliance player can turn in; 6029 is a Horde set-aside. No mutual-exclusion issue in the data (separate quest records, no excl links).
  4. 5126 classes=67. Bitmask decodes to Warrior+Paladin+Shaman (single bits 1/2/64 confirmed against known single-class quests), so only Warrior,Paladin,Shaman plus the Blacksmithing skill gate. Only two quests in the DB carry 67 (5126, 95217); both skill-gated the same way.
  5. 5124/5161/5162 contracted despite unobtainable pres. 5103's start object and 5160's giver have no spawn anywhere in the data, so the pres can never be taken — but 5124/5161/5162 themselves accept from placed Everlook NPCs. Contracted with honest structural pre notes (nothing invented); if spawn data appears, the pres route normally.
  6. 4901/93406/92009/5244 soft-pre minis. Their blocking pres (978, 93197, 92008, 5250) accept outside any contract but turn in here (Ranshalla, Vizzie, Nami, Wynd) — each gets a dedicated when onquest mini-step carrying its in-zone turn-in (and 979's accept at Erelas), the Arathi-79975 pattern. 978's own pre 3661 (Erelas, Teldrassil-side, uncontracted) is noted as the chain's true start: without it the mini skips cleanly.
  7. Cross-guide contracted pres are reference notes, not warnings. 3909 ← 3908 and 4842 ← 980 (both Ungoro-contracted), 4809 ← 4808 and 4810/4907 chain (Burning-Steppes-contracted), 5084 ← 5083 and 5086 ← 5085 (Felwood-contracted), 5244 ← 5249 (Felwood), 5252 ← 5248 and 6502 ← 6501 (WPL/Stormwind-contracted): the guides that own the pres turn them in; this guide just points at them.
