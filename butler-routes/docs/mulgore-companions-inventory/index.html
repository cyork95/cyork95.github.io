WHY THE TRIO SHIPS (Durotar distinction): the Durotar exclusion (276194/276198) rested on three legs - no rare role AND no loot AND no quest linkage. These three share only the first leg: each carries an eponymous drop with exactly one dropper anywhere in the data, in-band levels, and a tight patrol cluster. Exclusive named loot is the pack's signature-drop test, and it fires here. Quest-target check: none of the 9 is a kill objective, giver or turn-in of any quest in the data (full-record scan), and mulgore.guide carries no line for any of the 9 IDs - the Dun Morogh exclusion does not fire. Arra'chea (3058) is the lookalike: named, unflagged, a core quest target - stays out.

TAME LINES: one shipped (Mazzranache, argued above). Ghost Howl (wolf table) and The Rake (cat table) have drop evidence but no core-guide family corroboration - logged in docs/companion-unverified.md per the Durotar handling.
