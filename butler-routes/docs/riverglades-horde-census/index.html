Faction-ungating ruling (zone-specific judgment call)

The Alliance census recorded null classes/skill/event/repeatable across the zone — verified true for all 178 — but faction does NOT come from a null field: the records' races mask is the faction data (8589934770 = Horde, 4294967373 = Alliance, null = both), and the split on this key is 83/66/29 by that mask. Ruling: the 83 Alliance-mask quests are NOT acceptable by Horde players, so dropping the faction Alliance header alone would strand Horde players on dead steps. The ungate therefore gates every Alliance-mask quest line only Alliance (composing with existing gates; soft-pre when onquest minis and any travel lines by the same rule) and drops the header. Verified by the dead-line check from both factions and the travel-line search (see the ungating report); the Alliance contract's accepts/turn-ins are unchanged.
