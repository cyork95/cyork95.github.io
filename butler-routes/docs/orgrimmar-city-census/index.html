Orgrimmar City census (pack 0.97.0 round)

Every quest in the local data (questie-forever, --product wowf + QuestieDB) whose accept/start is in Orgrimmar (zone 1454): 318 candidates — 316 keyed by giver spawn (NPC or object on the 1454 map, dual-spawn = city NPC per the city rule), plus 6681 (the only item-start with an Orgrimmar source) and 97411 (zone 1454, empty start record — see BORDERLINE). No level band (city census): span is quest level 1–60 (min-levels 1–58), plus one special (1507, level -1, min 20 — the warlock Devourer of Souls ritual, same shape as Stormwind's 1716).

Disposition: 173 IN, 145 OUT of the candidates. The contract (docs/quest-contracts.json, slug orgrimmar-city, order 49, span 1-60) holds exactly the 173. Two further records examined but not candidates: 80325/95483 (turn-in-only in Orgrimmar, no accept anywhere — noted, not contracted).

Method

Accept/start decides: an NPC start with a 1454 spawn, an in-city object start, or an item start whose source is city-bound. A quest that merely turns in here but accepts elsewhere is not an Orgrimmar quest (80325, 95483). Zero overlap with zone contracts is verified (stormwind/elwynn, ironforge/dun-morogh, darnassus/teldrassil all overlap 0): quests already homed in Durotar stay out even where a giver dual-spawns, and are named below. Shared multi-city accepts go the other way — Stormwind, Ironforge and Darnassus each contract the same shared IDs for their own accepts (7562 in two cities, 8151/8233/8417 in two), so Orgrimmar contracts its own accepts of the same quests. Same-name same-chain twins both route (Hidden Enemies ×5; the three Earnest sets; Just Compensation/Saving the Best class sets). "Additional Runecloth" repeatables stay out (first-time donations only, mirroring all three Alliance city censuses).
