Redridge Mountains census (pack 0.19.0)

Accept-zone key: 1433 (same key space as Loch Modan's 1432). All 46 quests carrying zone=1433 were pulled; every one of their starters spawns only on 1433 (no dual-spawn starters — the 1:1 case). Eight quests from other zones whose givers also spawn on 1433 (1413 x8) stay with their home zones under the accept rule.

Band derivation: in-contract quest levels run 14–26 (entry messengers 120/143 at 14, WANTED posters 169/180 at 26); in-contract minLevels run 10–24, drawing the line at levels 14–26. The m24/L31 Dorin Songblade pair (95772/95795) is the in-band finale. Three L58–60 records run above the band and are contracted anyway by rule (see BORDERLINE): the accept-only 93551 (null min-level, Ironforge 95924/95925 precedent) and the item-starts 95816 (m1) and 98247 (m10).

No race header for the guide: no quest is race-locked (races are Alliance or null throughout) — per-line gates only, and only two classes occur (Warrior 1699/1702, Rogue 2282/2358). No profession quests (no skill-gated records). No event flags anywhere in the 46.
