BORDERLINE

  1. Band 40-52 vs 43-52. The brief expects mid-to-late 40s, but the data's minLevel cluster starts at 40 with 12 quests (Suntara, Prayer, Kalaran, Trinkets, Elemental LW). The band rule draws the TOP line; below-band entry is free. 40-52 keeps the cluster whole; 43-52 would orphan nothing (no quest is excluded by the low end either way) but would misstate where the zone starts. Picked 40-52.
  2. 8651 Lunar elder IN. The brief lists seasonal/event as OUT, but the Thousand Needles round contracted Lunar elders 8682/8724 as optional + seasonal note, lint-clean and verified. Consistency with the shipped pack beats the brief's generic rule; the note carries the seasonal caveat honestly. Butler rules on appeal.
  3. 5144 vs 5141/5143. Mutually exclusive LW variants (excl lists all three). 5141 starts in Feralas (1447), 5143 in TN/Feralas (1441/1444); 5144 starts with Sarah Tanner in SG. Chain-consistent SG variant contracted (94824/94817 + 7383/934 precedents); the other two stay out with their home zones named.
  4. 4264 + 3181 item-starts with qzone elsewhere. 4264's qzone is 242 and 3181's is 1432, but their start items drop in SG (78 Anvilrage droppers; Margol the Rager SG-only). The item-start rule keys on the source, not qzone — both IN, sequenced with sourcing notes so the route never wedges.
  5. 8997/8998 variants. The Alliance/Horde Bodley-finale pair would resolve to 8997 — except both sit at min 58, above the band, so the pair drops out together (8997 above-band, 8998 Horde set-aside).
  6. 7722 feeds repeatables but is itself one-time. Contracted: it is a normal pickup quest (pre for the rep cycle, not rep-flagged). The guide notes the plans have no placed source in the data.
  7. 3372's pre (3371 Dwarven Justice, Ironforge) and 4264's pre (4242 Abandoned Hope, Burning Steppes) sit outside the contract. Both accepts stay in the guide (their own accepts are in-zone); lint's structural pre-quest warnings are accepted with the chains named. Same for 3368 -> 3371 and 3481 -> 4022 links noted as out-of-scope pointers, and 4264 -> 4282.
