BORDERLINE (argued, none decided silently)

  1. The min28 bottom: Martek records 1108 (min28) and 1137 (min28) sit below the min30 bulk, but Martek spawns in-zone and the chain is genuine Badlands content (its pre 1106 lives in Thousand Needles, its turn-in leg for 1137 lands back in Thousand Needles — chains ending outside are IN). The band bottom is 28, not 30.
  2. The min1/lvl60 writs (94256, 94268, 94292, 94304): contracted IN per the Tanaris writ precedent and the pack's item-start rule — one-time records, all four turn-ins spawn in-zone, sequenced with bag notes so the route never wedges on the drop.
  3. Ribbly Screwspigot (4136) OUT: a Badlands-spawning giver is not enough — min48 is above the band by the mechanical rule, and the objectiveText sends the player to the Burning Steppes, the next zone east. If the band ever widens, this is the first record back.
  4. The Platinum Discs (2278) OUT as broken rather than IN as a finale: the min40 finale cluster would otherwise hold it, but an accept object with no spawns anywhere cannot be pointed at. This is a data gap, not a design call.
  5. Supplies and Studies (97663) and Mirage Mirror (97821) OUT as broken: both are Forever records (questie-forever source) with null minLevel; 97821 additionally has no accept. The Dustbowl objective text on 97663 reads real, so flag it for re-census if the record gains objectives.
  6. Seal of the Earth (779) OUT as repeatable while its chain stays IN: the one-time Tremors chain (718->732->717) completes the story; the rep=true turn-in cycle is the repeatable rule's exact target.
  7. Same-name twins 720/721 (A Sign of Hope) both IN: one object-start, one NPC follow-up — the Harvesting the Harvesters precedent.
  8. Out-of-contract pres (723 and 738 in Loch Modan's map but in no contract, 687 an Ironforge quest, 1106 a Thousand Needles quest): the guide routes the Badlands sides normally and reports the resulting structural warnings with the chain named; 4324 IS Tanaris-contracted but 4136 (its dependent) is out on band, so no link is needed in either direction.
  9. No class gates, no race gates: verified — every non-Horde record carries classes null, and the only races values present are null (neutral, available to all) and the Alliance mask. The guide needs no only lines for class or race.
