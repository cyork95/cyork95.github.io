The Prompt Kitchen used to live on its own domain, thepromptkitchen.fyi, on its own little stack. As of this month it lives here — [yorkdevelops.com/prompt-kitchen](/prompt-kitchen/) — as a section of this site, and the old domain is retired.

Why move it in? Honestly: one roof is easier to keep than two. The old setup carried a database and a serving stack for what is, at heart, a collection of text files. The new one is plain files on the same infrastructure as the rest of this site — faster to load, simpler to maintain, and nothing to log into. Everything the kitchen had made the move; the recipe library, the cookbooks, the meal-prep system prompts, the Pantry, and the guardrails are all here.

Three things are new since the move:

- **A freshness audit.** Every one of the recipes was checked against how current AI tools actually work. Most came through clean — they were written tool-agnostic on purpose. One genuinely broken recipe (it taught a Midjourney feature that no longer exists on the current version) was rewritten, and a handful of stale version references were cleaned up.
- **The Context Matters lessons.** Six short lessons on the skill that makes the biggest difference in prompting and gets taught the least: context. What to include, what to leave out, how to stop retyping the same background into every chat, and how to carry context between sessions without fooling yourself. They start at [Kitchen Prep](/prompt-kitchen/lessons.html) and take about five minutes each.
- **New recipes.** The library now holds 147 recipes, including a first batch written for how people actually work with AI in 2026: briefing agents, building a reusable context pack, grading output against a rubric, onboarding to an unfamiliar codebase, and storyboarding short video.

Two things are gone, deliberately: accounts and voting. The kitchen is a cookbook, not a social network — your favorites still save, right in your browser, and that's the whole feature list.

The kitchen stays a living section of the site from here: new recipes land in batches, and the library gets re-audited as the tools change. If you've never visited, the [Recipe Book](/prompt-kitchen/recipes.html) is the front door. If you're there to learn rather than cook, start with the [lessons](/prompt-kitchen/lessons.html).
